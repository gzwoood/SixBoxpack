HOW TO WORK WITH US

Good decisions start with clear details.

01 / DEFINE

Make the brief specific.

Send dimensions, product weight, quantity, intended use and your preferred visual direction. Reference images help clarify the structure and finish you have in mind.

02 / REVIEW

Look beyond the exterior.

Consider the insert, opening mechanism, surface feel and packing method. Discuss a sample to check how the product sits and how the complete presentation works.

03 / CONFIRM

Agree on the essentials.

Confirm materials, artwork, order quantity, quality requirements and delivery arrangements in writing before moving forward.
