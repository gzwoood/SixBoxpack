A softer silhouette with a traditional book-like feel. Plan cover and spine artwork together, allowing for the curve and wrapped edges.

Straight edges

A clean-lined profile for a restrained presentation. Review cover margins, the hinge area and the internal tray as one layout.

BUYER QUESTIONS

Book-shaped gift box FAQ

Can I customize the text and inside layout?

Yes. Share your cover artwork, inside-lid wording and internal configuration requirements. Confirm their final position and production method for your chosen material.

Are all book-style boxes covered in linen?

No. This featured collection uses linen fabric. Our wider book-style range below also includes other cover directions; confirm the material for the particular design.

Is a magnetic closure included?

Do not infer the closure from the exterior photo alone. Specify your preferred opening and closure, then confirm the construction with the sample.

Will the gift box protect my product during shipping?

The presentation box and shipping protection should be assessed separately. Discuss the outer carton, cushioning and any testing needed for your shipment.
