PACKAGING RESOURCES

A clearer brief. A better starting point.

Practical considerations for your next packaging project.

STRUCTURE

Rigid box or corrugated mailer?

A rigid box provides a firm presentation format for gifting and retail. A corrugated mailer is worth considering for delivery. Assess product weight, protection and the full shipping setup before choosing.

BRAND DETAILS

Plan the finish around your artwork.

Consider paper texture, printed color, foil and embossing together. Ask for a sample to assess legibility, contrast and how your chosen finish behaves on the selected paper.

PRODUCT FIT

Measure the product and its accessories.

Provide product dimensions and weight, along with any accessories. Allow room for inserts and confirm the fit with a physical sample, especially for jewelry and padel equipment.

BUYER FAQ

Before you start.

What should I include in a quotation request?

Share the box type, product dimensions, order quantity, artwork requirements, delivery destination and target date. Include reference images if you have a particular direction in mind.

Can I use the same packaging structure for different products?

A common exterior may suit a collection, but internal dimensions and inserts should be checked for each product. Consider protection, fit and the experience of opening the box.

How do I confirm materials and colors?

Specify your preferred materials and color references in your brief. Review samples and agree on the production specification before placing an order.

Where can I confirm minimum quantities and lead times?

These depend on the packaging structure, materials, finishes and order requirements. Request confirmation for your particular project rather than relying on a general estimate.
