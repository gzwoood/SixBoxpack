Magnetic Gift Boxes or Drawer Boxes? A Practical Guide for Apparel and Cosmetics

For clothing orders, we often start with a magnetic gift box. For cosmetics sets, a drawer box is another popular request. The right choice usually comes down to how the product fits, how it is packed and how you want the customer to open it.

By the sixboxpack packaging team

Magnetic gift boxes for clothing

A folded garment sits neatly in a hinged box, and the wide opening makes it easy to arrange tissue, a ribbon and a thank-you card. There is also room inside the lid for a short brand message.

Before choosing a size, fold the garment exactly as it will be packed. Measure that bundle, including any accessories. A shirt and a thick clothing set may look similar in a product listing, but they need very different box depths.

We recommend checking a packed sample. The lid should close comfortably without pressing down on the garment or leaving the contents loose. Confirm the magnetic closure as part of that sample; it is not visible in these photographs.

See our magnetic gift boxes and apparel packaging.

Need to save storage space? Ask for a foldable version.

Foldable boxes are worth discussing if you keep packaging in stock or need to ship empty boxes to a packing location. They arrive flat and are assembled before use. The sample here shows the corner tabs used to hold the sides in place.

Not every magnetic box folds flat. Tell us you need a foldable construction when requesting a quote, and ask for the flat packing dimensions as well as the finished box size. Your packing team should try the assembly before you approve the order.

Whether this reduces your overall cost depends on carton sizes, order quantity, freight and assembly time. We can provide the packing details so you can compare the options.

Drawer boxes for cosmetics sets

A drawer box opens by sliding the inner tray out of its sleeve. A ribbon pull makes the tray easy to grip, while an insert gives each item its own place.

For cosmetics, send us the dimensions of every bottle and jar, including the caps and pumps. Include applicators, cards and any other small items in the set. A layout that looks good empty still needs to work when the real products are inside.

The cutouts should hold the items in position and leave enough room to lift them out. On a sample, check both: can you remove the product comfortably, and does the loaded tray slide smoothly?

These photographs show an art-gift project, rather than a cosmetics order. The same drawer-and-insert approach can be adapted to a beauty set, with the insert material and cavity sizes chosen for the actual products.

Which would we choose?

Your priorityWhat to consider
A clothing gift presentationStart with a magnetic hinged box sized around the folded garment.
A set of bottles, jars or small itemsConsider a drawer tray with a fitted insert and easy product removal.
Less storage space before packingAsk about a foldable box and check assembly time.
A particular budgetCompare both structures at the same size, quantity, material and finish.

These are starting points from our projects. Drawer boxes can also work for clothing accessories, and magnetic boxes can hold cosmetics. We would rather check your product and packing needs than choose a box by industry alone.

Keep the branding brief clear

A simple logo may be all you need. If you want foil, printed artwork or inside-lid text, send the design with your enquiry. We will discuss the covering material and suitable process before sampling. You can see examples in our finishing guide.

For parcel delivery, also plan the outer shipping carton and cushioning. A presentation box alone should not be assumed to provide enough transit protection.

Send us what you plan to pack.

A product photo, dimensions, quantity and artwork are a good start. For clothing, add the folded size. For cosmetics, list each item in the set. Let us know your destination, target date and whether the boxes need to arrive flat.

We can then discuss the structure, prepare a quotation and arrange a sample for you to check.
