How We Manage Your Project

Use these eight stages to plan your packaging project. Timing and requirements are confirmed for each order.

01 Consultation

Share your product, dimensions, quantities and goals.

02 Design

Discuss structure, artwork and finishing options.

03 Quotation

Confirm pricing based on the agreed specifications.

04 Sampling

Review a sample and check the product fit.

05 Sample confirmation

Agree on revisions and approve the final specification.

06 Production

Confirm the production plan and quality requirements.

07 Packaging

Agree on packing, protection and shipping marks.

08 Delivery

Confirm the destination, shipping method and schedule.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

A better brief makes decisions easier.

Send what you already know. We can discuss the remaining details together.

Your product

Dimensions, weight, photos and any accessories. Tell us how the product should sit inside the packaging and whether it needs an insert.

Your brand

Logo files, artwork, color references and examples of the look you want. Identify which finishes are essential and which are optional.

Your order

Expected quantity, destination, target date and intended use. Mention whether you need retail presentation, gift packaging or a shipping format.

SAMPLE BEFORE APPROVAL

Check the fit. Review the finish.

Structure

Check the opening, closure, insert and internal space using the actual product where possible.

Appearance

Review the paper texture, artwork placement and color. Ask which details the proposed sample represents and which require separate confirmation.

Final specification

Record approved dimensions, materials, artwork version, packing and inspection requirements before confirming production.

CUSTOMIZATION FAQ

Useful questions before you order.

Can I combine different finishes?

Share the combination you have in mind. Compatibility depends on paper, artwork, structure and the production sequence, so confirm it for your project.

What artwork should I provide?

Send your available logo and artwork files, dimensions and color references. Ask which final file format and layout are needed before approval.

How are minimum quantities and timing confirmed?

They depend on the structure, materials, finishes and order quantity. Request a project-specific quotation and confirm the sample and production schedule.

Do the finish photographs guarantee the final result?

No. They provide a visual reference. Confirm the exact material, finish and sample for your own packaging before production.
