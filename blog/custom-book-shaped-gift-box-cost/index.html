How Much Do Custom Book-Shaped Gift Boxes Cost?

Custom book box prices by order quantity

For the reference configuration below, rounded-spine book boxes range from US$7.80 each at 100 pieces to US$1.90 at 1,000 pieces. Straight-edge book boxes range from US$6.50 to US$1.53 at the same quantities.

Box structure100 pcs300 pcs500 pcs1,000 pcs

All figures are indicative EXW prices in USD per box. Freight, import duties and taxes are excluded. Confirm the named EXW collection location and collection arrangements in your quotation. Final pricing depends on the approved artwork and specification.

What specification are these prices based on?

  • External dimensions: 24 × 20 × 5 cm. These are outer dimensions; confirm usable internal space for your product.
  • Covering: 120 gsm linen-textured paper on the outside and inside.
  • Board: 1200 gsm rigid cardboard.
  • Closure: two magnets.
  • Branding: one hot-foil logo or one screen-printed logo, with artwork details confirmed before production.

Linen-textured paper gives a woven-look surface at an accessible reference price. We can also offer linen fabric in different textures and other fabric options to suit your project. A different covering requires a separate quotation.

What changes the price of a custom book-shaped box?

1. Structure and dimensions

The rounded spine creates a traditional book-like profile, while a straight edge gives a crisp outline. The quoted configurations have different prices, so select the structure alongside your budget. Changes in size and construction also require a revised quotation.

2. Order quantity

Setup and production preparation are spread across more boxes at larger quantities. Compare the total order value as well as the unit price, and choose a quantity that fits your storage space and expected demand.

3. Covering and logo finish

The table includes linen-textured paper and one foil or screen-printed logo. Additional artwork, inside-lid text or finishes may change the price. Explore our materials and finishing options, then confirm the chosen effect on your actual covering with a sample.

4. Inserts and delivery requirements

A fitted insert, accessories or additional protective packing need to be specified separately. The EXW box price is not a delivered price: share your destination and shipping requirements when requesting a complete quotation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order 100 custom book boxes?

The reference table starts at 100 pieces. Confirm availability for your selected size, covering and artwork when requesting a quotation.

Is the linen fabric shown in the photos included in these prices?

The reference prices use 120 gsm linen-textured paper. Confirm your preferred covering with a physical sample. We can also quote linen fabric in different textures or other fabrics if you prefer.

Is a custom insert included?

The reference specification does not list a fitted insert. Send your product dimensions and preferred layout so we can quote any insert you need.

Are these final prices?

No. They are planning references for the stated configuration. Final EXW prices are confirmed after reviewing your artwork and complete requirements.
